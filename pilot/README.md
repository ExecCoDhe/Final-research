# Pilot within the final proposal

Status: **started; incomplete; no experimental results or independently adjudicated labels.** Updated 25 October 2026.

The [final proposal](../deliverables/final_proposal.pdf) remains the blueprint. Its page 20, Table 8 specifies an excluded pilot of five issuers and 30 claims; page 30 specifies the week-two feasibility milestone. This is not a replacement research topic or a new 30-case publication benchmark.

## What remains in scope

Exact entity/concept/period/unit/scope matching; original versus corrected disclosure authority; historical evidence availability; arithmetic and rounding; evidence-linked explanations; and justified uncertainty. The central research question is shorthand for these connected components.

The eventual 600-claim, 50-issuer study remains conditional on acquisition, annotation, and feasibility gates. Pilot issuers must be excluded from its development and test partitions.

## Protocol before implementation

1. Screen candidate disclosures and record exclusions. Count related line items and reporting periods from one correction event as one family, not independent correction events.
2. Preserve the original filing, corrective filing, correction note, exact table context, availability evidence, source URLs, and retrieval hashes.
3. Draft paired claims and record tentative labels separately from independent reviewer labels. Never resolve genuine ambiguity merely to balance classes.
4. Have an independent reviewer with financial-reporting competence inspect evidence and labels without seeing system predictions. Retain disagreements before adjudication.
5. Freeze pilot instructions before preparing the main corpus. Compare strong policy-aware simple rules using identical evidence. Do not infer algorithmic superiority from cases that those rules already solve.

The first family's original/latest switch, cutoff switch, and unchanged control illustrate the contract. They do **not** demonstrate that the proposed scoped resolver outperforms the proposal's B4 baseline. Establishing discriminating scoped or incomparable cases remains essential.

No research software or model implementation was created. Document parsing and data-inspection utilities were used to inspect public sources.
